Privacy Policy

Pi² by PaperChasers1  ·  Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

Pi² ("the App") is a peer-to-peer payment and marketplace application built on the Pi Network. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Pi², we may collect:

  • Pi Network identity data — your Pi username and user ID, provided by the Pi SDK upon authentication. We do not collect your password.
  • Transaction data — payment amounts, memos, and Pi transaction IDs associated with purchases made through the App.
  • Listing data — titles, descriptions, prices, and categories of items you list for sale.
  • Device data — basic device and browser information for security and performance purposes.

We do not collect fiat currency information, government IDs, or payment card numbers. All prices displayed in USD are informational reference only; no fiat currency is held or transmitted by Pi².

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and record Pi payments between users
  • To display your listings in the marketplace
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To improve the App's features and performance

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Pi Network — as required to process payments through the Pi SDK
  • Service providers — hosting and infrastructure partners under confidentiality agreements
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users

5. Data Retention

Transaction records are retained for up to 2 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to protect your Pi wallet credentials.

7. Children's Privacy

Pi² is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Pi² app or at our Pi Developer Portal page under @PaperChasers1.
